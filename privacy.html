Privacy

The short version: your files stay on your device.

Your files

Every tool on this site runs entirely in your web browser. Documents you open are read into your browser's memory, processed there, and downloaded back to your device. They are never transmitted to us or to any third party, and no copy is stored anywhere.

What we do not collect

We do not collect, see, store or analyse the contents of any file you process. We do not require an account, an email address or any personal information to use the tools.

Hosting

This is a static website. As with almost any website, the hosting provider may record basic technical information such as your IP address in server logs for security and reliability. We do not use that data to identify you.

Cookies and storage

No cookies are set and there is no tracking of any kind. A few small items are kept in your browser's own local storage, purely so the site feels familiar next time: your light/dark theme choice, the last few tools you used, and the settings you last chose on each tool. Clearing them does not affect your files.

Diagnostics log

The app keeps a local record of what it does — which tool ran, how long it took, file types and sizes, and any errors — so that a problem can be diagnosed rather than guessed at. This log is written to your browser's storage and is never transmitted anywhere: the site has no server that could receive it. File contents are never recorded, and file names are recorded only if you switch that option on. Passwords and similar secrets are filtered out and never written to it.

You can read, filter, copy, export or erase the whole log at any time, and switch recording off entirely, on the Settings page. It is deliberately not shown anywhere else in the app so that it never interrupts you, but it is always there when you want it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear on this page.
