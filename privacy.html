Privacy

The short version: your files stay on your device.

Your files

Every tool on this site runs entirely in your web browser. Documents you open are read into your browser's memory, processed there, and downloaded back to your device. They are never transmitted to us or to any third party, and no copy is stored anywhere.

What we do not collect

We do not collect, see, store or analyse the contents of any file you process. We do not require an account, an email address or any personal information to use the tools.

Hosting

This is a static website. As with almost any website, the hosting provider may record basic technical information such as your IP address in server logs for security and reliability. We do not use that data to identify you.

Cookies and storage

No cookies are set and there is no tracking of any kind. A few small items are kept in your browser's own local storage, purely so the site feels familiar next time: your light/dark theme choice, the last few tools you used, the settings you last chose on each tool, and the local diagnostics record described below. Clearing them does not affect your files.

Local diagnostics

The app keeps a small local record of tool runs and errors in your browser's storage, so that a fault can be diagnosed instead of guessed at. It is never transmitted anywhere — this site has no server that could receive it. It never contains the contents of any file, and file names are not included. Passwords and similar secrets are filtered out before anything is written. It is erased along with the rest of this site's local data when you clear your browser's storage for this site.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear on this page.
